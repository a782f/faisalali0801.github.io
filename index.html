About

I work on industrial automation and robotics — PLC programming, robot integration, and increasingly the machine-vision layer that tells a robot where to go. My recent focus is camera-to-robot calibration and learning-based perception on embedded hardware.

Before graduate school I spent two years in technical sales at TE Connectivity, supporting OEMs through line-down situations across connectors, sensors, and relays — so I'm comfortable talking engineering with a customer's technical team and translating it into terms the business side signs off on.
